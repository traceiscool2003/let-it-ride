ENEMY GRUNTS (shared by every regular enemy)

Record 3-5 short death grunts, one per file, each in a slightly different voice:
  grunt_death_1.ogg   "Ugh!"         (low, tough guy)
  grunt_death_2.ogg   "Argh!"        (mid, angry)
  grunt_death_3.ogg   "Not again..." (whiny)
  grunt_death_4.ogg   "Oof!"         (quick)
  grunt_death_5.ogg   "My chips!"    (high, panicked)

Drop them in this folder. The game picks one at random whenever an enemy goes down.
Full guide: HOW_TO_RECORD.md, section "Grunts and effort sounds".
